Multi-Channel Lead Sync & Webhook Architecture

Building real-time webhook endpoints connecting web forms to Salesforce Lead objects and automated Mailchimp email journeys.

ORGANIZATION

TOOLS & TECH

TIMEFRAME

May 2025 – Present

MEASURABLE IMPACT

40+ weekly manual entries eliminated

Manual Lead Ingestion & Duplicate Records

Client organizations acquiring inbound leads across custom landing pages, JotForm surveys, and event sign-up forms suffered from delayed follow-up times. Leads sat unassigned in form mailboxes, and manual copy-pasting into Salesforce introduced duplicate contacts and missing attribution fields.

Automated Multi-Channel Webhook Pipeline

At Cohby Consulting Services, I built an automated ingestion workflow:

  • Webhook Endpoint Ingestion: Form submissions trigger instant JSON payload POST requests to configured Zapier middleware.
  • Duplicate Matching & CRM Routing: Checks incoming email addresses against existing Salesforce Leads and Contacts using upsert logic — updates existing records, creates new Lead records with auto-assigned owner rules when there's no match.
  • Automated Email Journey Trigger: Pushes validated leads directly to Mailchimp audience segments, which fires a personalized welcome sequence shortly after form submission.
