THE CHALLENGE

Operational Bottlenecks & Fragmented Data

Clore Social Leadership develops leaders across the social sector in the UK and internationally. Managing multiple leadership cohorts involved tracking applicant data, program milestones, attendance, and feedback across fragmented external web forms, spreadsheet logs, and email lists.

Key operational challenges included:

  • Inconsistent Data Entry: Incomplete record creation led to inaccurate reporting on cohort demographics and completion rates.
  • Manual Tracking Overhead: Administrators spent significant weekly hours manually copy-pasting form responses into Salesforce and manually updating email marketing lists.
  • Low Program Visibility: Leadership lacked real-time visibility into participant progress, making early intervention difficult when participant engagement dropped.
THE APPROACH & ARCHITECTURE

Automated Flows, Validation Rules & API Integration

As Programs and CRM Administrator, I architected an automated ecosystem centered on Salesforce Sales Cloud:

1. Automated Salesforce Flow Workflows

Replaced manual record creation with triggered Salesforce Record-Triggered Flows and Scheduled Flows. When a participant status shifts, automated tasks, email notifications, and milestone stage updates trigger automatically.

2. Data Validation & Governance Rules

Implemented field-level validation rules enforcing standardized formatting, required custom field entries prior to stage transitions, and automated duplicate matching rules.

3. Multi-Channel Integration Pipeline

Configured Zapier webhook flows connecting JotForm online applications directly into Salesforce Contact and Application custom objects. Set up automated sync rules that update Mailchimp audience tags based on live Salesforce status updates.

4. Executive Analytics & Dashboards

Designed customized Lightning Dashboards and dynamic reports providing real-time tracking of cohort adherence, completion milestones, and engagement scores.

KEY TAKEAWAYS

Architectural Insights

Enforcing data integrity at the point of ingestion (via validation rules and clean API mappings) produces exponentially cleaner reporting downstream. Combining low-code Salesforce Flows with automated integration webhooks delivers enterprise-grade operational efficiency without heavy custom code maintenance.
