Security Governance & User Support Bottlenecks

AkamaFund needed tighter control over who could see what — sensitive financial data doesn't belong in front of every internal team. On top of that, a growing backlog of support tickets and customization requests was slowing down everyone's workflow improvements.

Least-Privilege Security Architecture & Customization Execution

As Salesforce Administrator, I implemented a least-privilege security model alongside a more structured user support framework:

  • Role Hierarchy & Permission Sets: Audited existing profiles and replaced monolithic profiles with modular Permission Sets and Field-Level Security (FLS) rules.
  • Customization Backlog Clearing: Designed custom objects, fields, page layouts, and automated validation rules, working through most of the pending business requests within the year.
  • End-User Training & Knowledge Base: Built out documentation and ran interactive training sessions to cut down on recurring support queries.

What changed

Days → Hours
Typical user support ticket resolution time
Most of the backlog
Pending Salesforce customization requests cleared

Stakeholder-facing dashboards also made it noticeably easier for non-technical teams to follow what the underlying metrics meant, without a Salesforce admin translating for them.
