Google Sheets to Salesforce Sync

Letting external suppliers keep working in Google Sheets while their data lands in Salesforce automatically, via Google Apps Script and the Salesforce REST API.

PROJECT TYPE

Supplier data integration

TOOLS & TECH

Google Apps Script, Salesforce REST API, Google Sheets

OUTCOME

Manual re-entry removed

The problem

External suppliers and collaborators without Salesforce licences were managing important operational data in Google Sheets. That left a gap between where they worked and the organisation’s CRM: data had to be re-entered by hand, and records often had gaps.

What I built

I built a Google Apps Script integration that syncs what suppliers enter in Google Sheets straight into Salesforce through the REST API. It runs on form submission or on a schedule, so suppliers never need access to the Salesforce org and the CRM data stays current.

What changed

Manual re-entry stopped entirely, external collaborators kept a simple working environment, and Salesforce records stayed in step with supplier data.
