MEASURABLE IMPACT

Parses & tailors in under a minute

Generic Resumes Rejected by ATS Filtering

Modern recruitment relies heavily on Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) that automatically scan resumes for exact keyword density matching target job postings. Applicants spending hours manually rewriting bullet points often miss critical terminology, resulting in automated rejections.

NLP Parsing & Prompt Engineering

I developed ATS Resume Tailor to automate custom resume alignment:

  • Job Description Parser: Extracts key hard skills, required certifications, and action verbs using NLP keyword extraction models.
  • Resume Match Scoring: Compares user resume content against target job requirements, calculating a keyword alignment percentage and missing skill report.
  • Intelligent Bullet Generation: Prompts LLM models to reframe existing user accomplishments using exact target terminology without exaggerating or inventing experience.

Performance

Parses a job posting, scores the resume against it, and generates tailored bullet points in well under a minute — fast enough to use live while applying, rather than as a separate step you do the night before.
