Screen Flows for Business Processes & User Training

Guiding staff step by step through key processes inside Salesforce with Screen Flows that double as in-app training.

PROJECT TYPE

Process guidance & training

TOOLS & TECH

Salesforce Screen Flows, Flow Builder

OUTCOME

Fewer errors, faster onboarding

The problem

Staff were working through complex business processes by hand with no guided structure. That led to inconsistent data entry, process errors and slow onboarding for new users.

What I built

I designed and deployed a series of interactive Screen Flows and in-app guidance that take staff through key business processes step by step, directly inside Salesforce. They double as training tools, so people rely less on external documentation.

What changed

Manual process errors went down, data entry became consistent across the team, and onboarding time for new users dropped significantly.
