THE OPERATIONAL CHALLENGE

Scaling Process Bottlenecks & Legacy Data Spreadsheets

Correlation One operates large-scale data science and tech training programs globally. As candidate volume and partner operations expanded, cross-departmental handoffs relied heavily on manual spreadsheet trackers and ad-hoc email approvals.

Specific pain points included:

  • Approval Delays: Discount requests and custom partner contracts stalled in email threads, causing 3 to 5 day approval delays.
  • Siloed Spreadsheets: Legacy candidate records were stored across unstandardized CSV files with inconsistent formatting.
  • User Interface Friction: Default page layouts exposed 40+ unnecessary fields to end users, slowing down data entry and increasing entry errors.
THE TECHNICAL SOLUTION

Workflow Engineering & Data Migration

Over 15 months as Salesforce Administrator, I restructured core operational workflows inside Salesforce:

1. Automated Approval Processes & Flows

Designed and configured 22 automated Flow processes and multi-step approval paths. Discount approvals and partner contract sign-offs were routed directly to manager queues with dynamic email & in-app notifications.

2. Data Loader Cleansing & Migration

Extracted, deduplicated, and transformed over 15,000 legacy records using Salesforce Data Loader. Standardized state/country picklists, validated phone numbers, and enforced strict external ID matching to ensure zero record loss.

3. Tailored Lightning App Layouts

Built role-tailored Lightning App pages for admissions, partner management, and support teams — hiding redundant fields and placing key action buttons directly at the top of record pages.
