Flow Automation UK leadership-development charity

Automated Survey Follow-Up Framework

PROBLEM STATEMENT

Survey responses from Jotform sat unlinked to participants, there was no single view of who had completed what, and staff chased non-respondents by hand from spreadsheets.

TECHNICAL APPROACH

Designed a hub-and-spoke Follow Up object and six layers of Salesforce Flow (create, link, track, chase, sync, reconcile), applied to every survey type across more than 35 active flows.

MEASURABLE OUTCOME

Every response is linked to its participant as it arrives, completion for up to 9 surveys sits in one place, and non-respondents are chased automatically: four reminders, then escalation to the programme lead.
