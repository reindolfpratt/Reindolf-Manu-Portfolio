ABOUT ME

Started in operations. Ended up building the systems operations teams depend on.

I didn't set out to become a Salesforce administrator. I started managing field research projects in Ghana, moved into CRM administration in the UK almost by accident, and kept following the parts of the job I found most interesting: the automation, the data, the "why does this take three days" questions.

10+ years across CRM, operations & data

I'm a Salesforce Administrator and data analyst based in Ottawa, ON. Day to day, that means untangling messy CRM setups, building automations that hold up under real use, and occasionally digging into a dataset to answer a question leadership actually cares about. I also co-founded Cohby Consulting Services, a small Ontario-registered consultancy where a colleague and I take on Salesforce, automation, and AI projects for clients directly.

I've worked this way across the UK, Canada, and Ghana, and the pattern's been consistent: manual processes pile up until someone automates them, CRM adoption dies without proper training, and raw data is only useful once someone bothers to turn it into something a non-technical stakeholder can act on. That's the work I keep gravitating toward.

I'm finishing an MSc in Computer Science with Data Science at the University of Wolverhampton, and I hold the Salesforce Certified Administrator, Salesforce Certified Sales Cloud Consultant, and Professional Scrum Master (PSM I) certifications.
