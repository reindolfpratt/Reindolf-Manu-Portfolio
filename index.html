How I actually approach this stuff.

No two Salesforce orgs or n8n workflows look the same, but the way I work doesn't change much:

Find the actual bottleneck

I ask a lot of "why" before I open Setup or a Flow builder. Half the time the real fix isn't the one people first ask for.

Build things that fail gracefully

Automations break. The question is whether they break loudly, in a way someone notices before it costs a week of bad data.

Show my work

I'd rather tell you honestly what improved and by roughly how much than dress up a project with numbers that sound better than they are.
